# floatey support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: shaydon.bodemar@gmail.com

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Go to **General > Keyboard > Keyboards**.
3. Tap **Add New Keyboard…** and choose **floatey**.

In any text field, tap the **globe** key until floatey appears.

## Common questions

- **floatey doesn't appear in some fields.** Password fields, phone-number pads and apps that turn off third-party keyboards always use the standard keyboard.
- **Does floatey need Full Access?** No. It never asks for it, and nothing you type leaves your iPhone.
- **How do I type with one hand?** Tap the arrow at either end of the row that starts with **A**. Tap the arrow beside the keyboard to go back to both hands.
- **How do I split the keyboard on the iPhone Duo?** On the inner display in landscape, press both arrows beside the **A** row at the same time.

The floatey app has a full guide and its settings.
