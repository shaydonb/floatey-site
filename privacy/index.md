# floatey privacy policy

Applies to floatey 1.0 and later until this page says otherwise. Effective November 12, 2026. Published versions: [1.0](1.0/).


floatey doesn't collect, store off your device, or share anything you type.

- **No network access.** The keyboard runs without Full Access, so iOS doesn't let it reach the internet or share data with other apps. The floatey app makes no network requests either.
- **What stays on your device:** your settings (corrections, capitals, predictive text, arrow hold time, double tap, haptics), whether the keyboard was last one-handed or split, how often you've used each emoji, the word choices floatey learns from (which word you picked over which, and the word before it, for up to 30,000 pairs), and your vocabulary: each word you type and how often you've used it, for up to 5,000 words, so names and words floatey doesn't know can be swiped and aren't corrected away. These are stored only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete floatey.
- **What floatey never keeps:** the text you type, unless you turn on Diagnostics. Your vocabulary counts single words, never sentences or the order you typed them in. Fields that turn off corrections, like web addresses and email addresses, aren't learned from. Swipe typing, corrections and suggestions work from word lists built into the app, your vocabulary, and what's around the cursor while you type.
- **Contacts and text replacements:** iOS gives every keyboard the names from your contacts and your text replacements, so they can be typed and corrected. floatey reads them while the keyboard is open and doesn't store them. It has no access to the rest of your contacts.
- **Diagnostics, off unless you turn it on:** a testing switch in the app's Settings. While it is on, the keyboard keeps a list of its recent touches and the keys they typed in its own memory. Tapping **Log** in the suggestion bar types the list into the text field and clears it, and it is also cleared when the keyboard restarts. It never leaves your iPhone unless you paste it somewhere.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.**

If you join the beta through TestFlight, Apple shares the crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under [Apple's TestFlight privacy terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

If this ever changes, this page will change first.
