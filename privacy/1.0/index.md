# floatey privacy policy

This is the policy as published for floatey 1.0, effective November 12, 2026. [Current policy](../).


floatey doesn't collect, store off your device, or share anything you type.

- **No network access.** The keyboard runs without Full Access, so iOS doesn't let it reach the internet or share data with other apps. The floatey app makes no network requests either.
- **What stays on your device:** your settings (arrow hold time, double tap, haptics), whether the keyboard was last one-handed or split, and your recently used emoji. These are stored only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete floatey.
- **What floatey never keeps:** the text you type, unless you turn on Diagnostics. Swipe typing and suggestions work from a word list built into the app and look only at what's around the cursor while you type.
- **Diagnostics, off unless you turn it on:** a testing switch in the app's Settings. While it is on, the keyboard keeps a list of its recent touches and the keys they typed in its own memory. Tapping **Log** in the suggestion bar types the list into the text field and clears it, and it is also cleared when the keyboard restarts. It never leaves your iPhone unless you paste it somewhere.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.**

If you join the beta through TestFlight, Apple shares the crash reports and feedback you choose to send with the developer, under [Apple's TestFlight privacy terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/).

If this ever changes, this page will change first.
